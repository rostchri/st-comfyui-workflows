ComfyUI Workflows
Talks to a self-hosted ComfyUI backend running SaladTechnologies/comfyui-api. Workflows and their parameter schemas are discovered dynamically via /api-proxy/workflows-meta and /api-proxy/docs/json. Each request fires from the browser directly to the Base-URL with credentials: 'include' (works with cookie-based SSO).
